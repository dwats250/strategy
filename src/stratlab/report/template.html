Overview

Equity and drawdown

Account equity at each session close. Click a variant to show or hide it.

Metrics and deltas

Portfolio rows use session-close account equity (Sharpe/Sortino annualized with √252 over all sessions, flat days included). Trade rows use per-trade R multiples and are never annualized. Δ = variant − baseline under the same window, capital and costs.

Full history, subperiods and the recent window

Each row is a separate backtest started with fresh capital on the window's first session; indicators are warmed on prior history. Same settings and costs in every window.

Monthly returns and stability

Monthly returns and rolling 63-session Sharpe come from the full-history run (running equity, no reset).

Trades and price inspection

Full-history trades. Search, sort and filter change the VIEW only; no statistic is recalculated. Tap a trade to draw its session.

What each variant changes

Trades matched to the baseline by signal bar and side (full-history window). Independent strategies are not diffed against the baseline.

Concentration in large winners

Curve: cumulative net P&L with trades sorted from best to worst. A result that only exists because of a few trades rises steeply then falls for most of the curve.
