Initial risk under notional sizing money at risk to the stop

Each entry uses 99.9% of equity as notional, so the money at risk to the initial stop varies by trade. These are exposure facts, not performance.

Initial risk = shares × |fill − stop| ÷ equity before the entry minute. Actual reward/risk = side × (target − fill) ÷ |fill − stop|. The VWR3 target is placed from the signal close.
