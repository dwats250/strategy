## 4. Reserve rules (the "wait" rule)

1. **Reserve ≠ allocation.** Its default state is undeployed; its purpose is the earmarks.
2. **Earmarks (caps, first-trigger-first-served):** EWZ ≤ US$400 · Stage A duration (MBB) ≤ US$300 · one tactical position ≤ US$400 notional · asymmetric budget ≤ US$100 per 12 months. **Floor:** US$150 stays in SGOV.
3. **Logging:** a trigger counts only once logged (date, evidence, source) in `ROUNDS.md` or `WATCHLIST.md` before the order.
4. **No averaging down** tactical or regime positions.
5. **Time rule:** on **Apr 1, 2027**, if < 25% of the reserve has been deployed and no trigger is pending, move half of the undeployed balance into **VT** (US-listed global equity; no FX round trip). Repeat on Oct 1, 2027 for the remainder.
6. **Drawdown rule:** if XEQT falls ≥ 15% from fill, up to US$300 of the reserve may buy VT; BN add per §3.2. Strategic buying in a drawdown is the reserve's best use.
7. **Asymmetric budget trigger (all three):** (a) HYG 30-day IV rank < 30; (b) HY OAS widened ≥ 50 bp over the prior four weeks; (c) BBB OAS > 1.20% **or** a public credit vehicle suspends redemptions / trades > 10% below NAV. Then: puts ≥ 6 months out, ~8% OTM, ≤ US$100; sell half at 3×, rest at 5×; time stop at 30 days to expiry. Rationale for dropping the Round 1 hedge is in `ROUNDS.md` (EV ≈ 0.73× premium).

## 5. What would change this proposal

- **Bond trend turns** (10Y weekly close below its 50-day with real yields off their highs): Stage A opens — MBB from the reserve (`WATCHLIST.md` §1).
- **Term-premium relief** (MOVE < 90, clean long auctions, 30Y real < 3.0%): Stage B — TLT.
- **Brazil result + confirmation:** EWZ per §3.4.
- **Credit shock** (HY OAS ≥ 4%): asymmetric budget may already be live; BN review triggers; XEQT drawdown rule may apply; a flight-to-quality TLT exception applies (`WATCHLIST.md` §1).
- **Inflation regime** (10Y breakeven > 2.6% with core accelerating): duration stays closed; TIPS before nominals.
- **Peace deal / oil collapse:** no portfolio change (no energy or tanker position).
