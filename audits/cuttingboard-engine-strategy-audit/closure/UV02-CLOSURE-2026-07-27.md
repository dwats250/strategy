- A List of Trades contains only trades that were taken; rejections never become trades. This
  is why the EA trace schema preserves every evaluation opportunity — accepted, rejected, and
  halted.

  manifest freezes source, config, dataset, environment, and schema identities before the run.

FULL-history window, friction `UNRECOVERABLE`. It is not a baseline, not a benchmark, not a
prior, and not a verdict on the implemented engine. `UV02_STUDY_CONTRACT.md` §7 already
prohibits the claim classes involved, and that prohibition stands unchanged.
